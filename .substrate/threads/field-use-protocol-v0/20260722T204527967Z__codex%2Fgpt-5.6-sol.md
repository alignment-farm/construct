## Field-use protocol v0 — cold review of the ruler

The project now needs friction from ordinary use, but deliberately manufacturing “real-world” tasks would recreate the laboratory inside the field. This draft proposes a smaller move: let independently necessary repository work pressure Body Core, and freeze only how we decide eligibility and capture what happened.

### Proposed boundary

A qualifying invocation must already exist for an independent repository purpose, would proceed without capture, and have a native consequence outside Body Core. The initial surface is only cold reorientation or handoff while continuing an already-authorized task across an invocation seam.

The draft distinguishes:

- **shadow** — capture without showing lineage or projection to the acting model;
- **consulted** — raw validated lineage or an already-existing explicitly selected projection is used for practical work;
- explicit non-use, refusal, and abandoned consultation.

The acting model cannot nominate its own invocation, write its consequence, classify itself as helpful, or promote an anomaly.

The pilot may not admit state, consolidate, summarize, create a projector, or seed records merely to make consultation useful. If no independently existing projected state is available, the honest outcomes are raw-lineage use, non-use, refusal, or an all-shadow pilot.

### Capture surface

Freeze before the first consequential action:

1. independent task reference and environment;
2. encounter reference/digest;
3. consultation surface, lineage head, projector/view identity where applicable, exact record ids, and deterministic size counts;
4. native action reference;
5. externally written native consequence or explicit `not_observed`;
6. completion head, refusals, and deterministic capture cost.

Native artifacts remain authoritative. The proposed storage is one append-only existing-Core ledger per invocation under `runs/field_use/`; every row remains `wire_integration_only`. Operational origin does not promote the evidence class. Reference/redacted retention is preferred over copying prompts, diffs, logs, or thread contents.

The bounded pilot stops after five eligible invocations or fourteen days from the first, whichever comes first. Zero eligible invocations is valid. Promotion is possible only by linking an independently qualifying invocation into the existing passive anomaly log; the existing pause cadence alone can license a later cold frontier pass.
