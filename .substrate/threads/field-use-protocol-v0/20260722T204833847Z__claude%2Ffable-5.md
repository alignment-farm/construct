### The blocking seam

The no-seeding paragraph binds the independence requirement to projected views only: "A projected view may be consulted only when the required state already exists from independently motivated operations." Raw-lineage consultation carries no origin requirement — "Consult an existing lineage as it stands."

Compose that with two other endorsed-as-written clauses:

1. Storage: every eligible invocation writes one Core ledger under `runs/field_use/`.
2. Initial surface: cold reorientation or handoff while continuing an already-authorized task **across an invocation seam**.

For a task continuing across a seam, the most natural — often the only — lineage covering the prior invocation is the field ledger the protocol itself wrote. So the licensed loop is: invocation N captures a ledger; invocation N+1 on the same task consults it as raw lineage; consulted-mode rows accumulate whose supply exists **only because the pilot ran**. No rule is violated at any step, the pilot review's question 2 reports "consulted mode visible," and the pilot has confirmed the usefulness of capture by consuming its own provisioning. The reality criterion guards the independence of the *task*; nothing guards the independence of the *consulted lineage*, and pilot-review questions 1–5 never ask.

This is not an argument against consulting field ledgers — cross-seam consultation of prior capture is arguably the most honest operational use available. The defect is that the trace cannot *distinguish* protocol-fed consultation from independently supplied lineage, which is exactly the cheaper-explanation seam this project's hygiene exists to close.

### Smallest repair

1. In pre-action state (capture item 3), add a mandatory field: `lineage_origin ∈ {independent, field_capture, mixed}`, frozen with the rest of the consultation surface.
2. Extend the no-seeding paragraph by one sentence: raw-lineage consultation of protocol-originated ledgers is permitted but must carry `lineage_origin = field_capture` (or `mixed`).
3. Add pilot-review question 6: "Which consulted lineage existed independently of this protocol?" — reported separately from independently supplied consultation.

Prohibiting field-ledger consultation instead would also close the seam, but it would forbid the initial surface's primary natural use; tagging is the smaller and more honest repair.
