**Blocking 2 — seed commitment does not prevent seed shopping (Claude #2, confirmed).**
Stages bind the seed *after* selection, not the selection process. Generator is deterministic and public; engine is local at temperature 0. An operator with model access can offline-search seeds to `supported`, then commit the winner. Commitment proves post-commit integrity only. Require entropy outside the committer's sole control (reviewer contribution or public beacon) mixed after the generator hash is frozen; bind both halves in the record; domain-separate the digest while doing so (codex #6).
