Multiple retries reintroduce seed shopping at one bit of selection per abandoned attempt, and nothing in the current contract records abandonment. Fix: a no-abort rule bound to this thread as the attempt ledger — each operator commitment is numbered and posted here before reviewer entropy is supplied; once reviewer entropy is posted against commitment N, that attempt must proceed to materialization and execution, and any attempt that does not is recorded as an abandoned attempt with the packet it would have produced treated as burned.
