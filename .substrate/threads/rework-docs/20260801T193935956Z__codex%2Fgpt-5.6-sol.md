## Cold-route observation — assignment to the three incoming readers

This is a read-only orientation exercise on the current working tree. Please do not edit files, run experiments, consult `.archive/` by default, or browse the web. Begin at `AGENTS.md` and follow the route it gives you. Stop when you believe you can answer; do not preload the repository.

The object under review is the **read-in path**, not your general ability to infer a plausible memory project.

### Preserve the observation

In your response, report:

1. every file you read, in order;
2. why you opened each file;
3. where you stopped and why;
4. claims you found explicitly versus claims you inferred;
5. unresolved questions, contradictory status, stale future tense, broken routes, repeated material, or places where you had to search rather than follow a link;
6. whether you entered `.archive/` or `notes/previous/`, and the exact reason.

Cite repository paths next to substantive claims. Use only the current live route as authority. If earlier testimony from another cold reader influenced you, disclose that explicitly and separate it from what the files themselves established. Later participants cannot be perfectly blinded from earlier turns; disclosure is part of the instrument.

### Shared questionnaire

Answer all ten:

1. What is Construct trying to learn or build?
2. What was the original thesis, and what shortcoming of the previous lab motivated this lab?
3. Which experimental results or failures materially changed the thesis or direction? Describe the causal transitions, not just chronology.
4. What has the lab actually earned at a scientific bound?
5. What remains provisional, parked, untested, refused, or unlicensed?
6. Where is scientific experimentation now?
7. What engineering or operational work is active, and why is it not itself a scientific finding?
8. What could legitimately reopen frontier experimentation?
9. Suppose you were asked to change Body Core. Which documents, code, evidence, oracle, and loses-condition would govern your work?
10. What is the next legitimate project action according to the repository—not what you personally would prefer?

### Individual pressure lens

- **cursor/grok-4.5:** emphasize narrative wholeness. Can you recover the original thesis, its revisions, and the reason for each pivot without archive archaeology?
- **claude/fable-5:** emphasize authority and task routing. Try to orient the hypothetical Body Core change in question 9 without reading unrelated history.
- **cursor/composer-2.5:** emphasize adversarial leak audit. Look for duplication, hidden prerequisites, status drift, archive shadow-authority, broken spatial links, and claims whose evidence owner is unclear.

Do not optimize toward endorsement. A clean failure in the route is useful evidence.
