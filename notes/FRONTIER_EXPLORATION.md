# Frontier exploration and claim promotion

Status: **ACTIVE from 2026-08-03; supersedes
[FRONTIER_PAUSE.md](FRONTIER_PAUSE.md) for new work**.

This policy restores deliberate experimental contact while preserving the
lab's evidence boundary. The governing distinction is:

> Discovery may be messy; promotion may not be.

The former pause correctly protected the record from weak claims, but it also
made near-isolation a prerequisite for exploration. That selected for questions
already unusually observable, naturally occurring, externally gradable, and
nearly ready for validation. Construct now places rigor at the transition from
exploration to claims rather than using it to prevent contact.

This is an operating-policy change, not a scientific finding and not a reversal
of any prior experimental verdict.

## Two lanes

### Exploration

Exploration exists to find phenomena, break mechanisms, discover useful
measurement surfaces, and learn which questions deserve validation.

It may:

- author fixtures and synthetic worlds;
- contact engines, including open-weight and hosted models;
- inspect exact prompts, offers, outputs, tool traces, and failures;
- iterate on memory representations, projectors, prompts, and provisional
  scorers;
- use engineering regressions and historical episodes as design inputs;
- stop on null, incoherence, cost, or lack of treatment contact without issuing
  a typed experimental refusal.

Every exploration must have a short charter that states:

1. the practical or scientific question;
2. why the work is worth doing without a positive result;
3. the engines, fixtures, and mutable surfaces initially in scope;
4. a call, time, or iteration budget;
5. the records that will be retained;
6. a stopping rule;
7. the conditions required before promotion may be proposed.

Exploratory artifacts and rows must be labeled `exploratory_only`. They may not
be written into authoritative experimental ledgers under `runs/`, added to the
scientifically earned table, or cited as evidence that a memory mechanism
works. They may support engineering decisions and motivate a validation
proposal.

Iteration history stays visible. Failed or inconvenient calls may not be
silently removed from exploratory summaries. A charter may be amended while
work is active, but the amendment must identify what changed and which earlier
outputs informed it.

### Validation

Validation exists to test one bounded causal claim. It retains Construct's
standing experimental discipline:

- one engine forked across memory conditions;
- foreground, model settings, tools, and oracle fixed across branches;
- harness-authored offers, diffs, and verdicts;
- an external answer or consequence oracle;
- at least one condition where the mechanism should lose;
- computed outcomes rather than model self-report or inspection alone;
- complete reporting of admitted cells and refusals.

A successful exploration does not become validation by changing its label.
Promotion requires a new reviewed specification and one of these prospective
boundaries:

1. untouched fixtures or episodes sealed before validation contact;
2. a prospectively collected natural episode set with fixed inclusion rules;
3. a newly generated set from a frozen generator whose seeds are committed
   before model contact.

Fixtures, prompt variants, seeds, or outcomes inspected during exploration are
development data. They may explain the proposal but may not score the promoted
claim. If no honest prospective boundary exists, the work remains exploratory.

## Relationship to the anomaly log

The [anomaly log](ANOMALY_LOG.md) remains useful for raw observations arising
from unrelated work. It no longer gates authored fixtures, engine contact, or
exploratory mechanism work. Natural observations and deliberate exploration
must remain distinguishable; an authored probe may not be entered as a passive
anomaly.

The former monthly cadence and reopening funnel remain historical records in
the superseded pause. They no longer decide whether exploration can begin.

## Relationship to field use

The [field-use ruler](field-use/README.md) keeps its sealed independence and
non-interference rules. Exploratory GM calls, authored fixtures, and deliberate
memory consultation are not eligible field-use invocations. Field-use traces do
not become exploratory branches, and exploration does not advance the pilot.

## First exploration

The first work authorized under this policy was the bounded
[GM memory exploration](GM_MEMORY_EXPLORATION.md). It closed
`exploratory_lead`, not a finding, after repeatable contact on authored
development fixtures. Its contacted cases remain development data. A later
validation must use a fresh prospective boundary and explicitly fixed sampling.

## Non-claims

This policy does not establish that:

- looser search produces better mechanisms;
- any prior refused candidate would have worked;
- fixtures predict field behavior;
- open-weight behavior transfers to hosted engines;
- exploratory scores are estimates of an earned effect;
- Body Core or any other provisional architecture is validated.

It changes how questions are allowed to form. It does not lower the standard
for what the project may claim to know.
