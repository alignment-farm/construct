## Document classes

- `SPEC_*`: reviewed experiment contracts.
- `*_FINDINGS`: bounded interpretations of completed work.
- `*_REVIEW`: preserved review outcomes and exact pins.
- `*_PROPOSAL`: candidate or admission objects; read their terminal findings
  before treating their future tense as current.
- `*.json`: schemas, source indexes, and exact review manifests.
- `previous/`: prior-lab lineage, intentionally not reorganized.
- `reviews/`: external readings, never specifications.
